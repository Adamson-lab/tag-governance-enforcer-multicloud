# ADR-0005: Auto-fix only deterministic changes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Deciders:** Architecture board (author: Favour Adamson)
- **Reversibility:** two-way door

## Context
A wrong owner or cost-center is worse than a missing one.

## Decision
Auto-fix: alias/case normalization and inheritance of inheritable keys. Never invent values; missing owner/classification goes to ticket or escalation.

## Alternatives considered
Fix by guessing from naming or last modifier: rejected, error-prone and politically costly.

## Consequences
Lower auto-fix rate, higher trust.
