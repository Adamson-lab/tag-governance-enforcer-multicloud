# ADR-0010: Expired sandbox assets are stopped and snapshotted, never auto-deleted

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Deciders:** Architecture board (author: Favour Adamson)
- **Reversibility:** two-way door

## Context
Deleting on a date mismatch can destroy work.

## Decision
EXPIRY_ENFORCE stops/deallocates after approval, snapshots, and deletes only after a second grace period.

## Alternatives considered
Immediate delete: rejected, irreversible. Do nothing: rejected, orphaned spend.

## Consequences
Some residual cost during grace periods.
